How MedReport AI Evaluates Reports

01
Document Ingestion

Files are securely uploaded and stored in Microsoft Azure Blob Storage with unique identifiers.

02
Text & Layout OCR

Azure Document Intelligence extracts text, tables, and numeric lab readings from uploaded files.

03
Reference Comparison

Compares patient values against clinical standard reference intervals to highlight potential anomalies.

Clinical Evaluation Note

This application is designed as an educational clinical decision support tool to help patients understand complex laboratory reports. Laboratory findings should always be clinically correlated by a certified healthcare professional.
